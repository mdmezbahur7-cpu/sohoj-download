Free hosting limits

One job runs at a time, with a shared queue of up to 10 jobs and up to 2 active jobs per browser. Files are limited to approximately 512 MB per job, with 15 minutes of processing time. Available disk space and platform rate limits apply. There is no daily subscription quota. Inactive free hosting may sleep, so the first visit can take a minute to wake up.

Which platforms work?

Supported inputs include public links from YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Reddit, Twitch, Pinterest, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and Bilibili. Not every link will work. Platforms may block data-center downloads or require login. Private, DRM-protected, paywalled media, playlists and livestreams are excluded.

Download failed?

Try a shorter public video or lower quality. If the source requires login or blocks the server, this app cannot bypass it. An error is shown in Download activity. Browser history is not a backup of the files: expired files need a new download.
